## Decisions

- **Vertical slice seeds 4 of the 8 GDD animals** (rabbit, chicken, deer, wolf). Boar/bears/red variants need the M7 perception + threat machinery to be non-decorative; the FSM + spawn/loot/cap machinery is complete and kind-data-driven, so each added animal is a one-line content entry (GDD §12: "Adding … requires a test scenario" — added with the AI milestone).
- **Melee animal damage is `round(8 × toolMultiplier)`**, reusing the gather swing reach/cooldown so M5 hunting stays consistent with M2 gathering; the dedicated combat formulas (falloff, hit zones, armor sums) are M6's territory (GDD §11/§25).
- **Food scrubs 20 rads** (GDD §15 "Food, Water Bottles and Anti-Radiation Pills remove fixed amounts"; water bottles are M8 containers).
- **Radiation on the wire is the session's own value only** (GDD §22: a client never sees another player's vitals beyond the body); the join-time top-level field covers the baseline, own deltas carry it afterwards.
- **Client shows the radiation number next to the bar** — a deliberate deviation from GDD §6 "the exact number is not shown by default": the M5 gate must verify the wire value, and a 1-line HUD deviation is cheaper than a separate debug mode; the bar/color is the primary readout.
- **M4's `rest` (1 hp sleeping-bag regen) is kept as-is**; the full sleep channel is not in M5's GDD scope ("Sleep" is an M7 AI state and the GDD has no player sleep system in §6), so no new channel was invented.
